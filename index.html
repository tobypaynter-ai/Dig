Whether you're 17 and fresh out of school or 45 and looking for a career change, our two-week intensive programme puts you in the cab from day one, learning by doing — not from a textbook.

What You'll Learn

The Full Course Programme

Our structured two-week course takes you from zero to examination-ready, covering everything a professional excavator operator needs to know.

🔧

Machine Familiarisation

Get to know your machine inside out — controls, safety checks, pre-start inspections, and daily maintenance across our 8 excavators.

⛏️

Basic Excavation Tasks

Start with fundamental digging, levelling, and loading tasks. You master each skill before progressing — no shortcuts, no rushing.

📐

Trenching & Grading

Accurate trenching for utilities and precise grading — the bread-and-butter skills that keep you in constant demand on any site.

🦺

Site Safety & Legislation

Understand site hazards, safe working zones, overhead cables, buried services, and your legal responsibilities as an operator.

🔄

Loading & Slewing

Efficient load-and-slew techniques with precision and speed — exactly what foremen look for when hiring plant operators.

🏆

CPCS Final Examination

Your two-week programme culminates in a formal CPCS-registered examination. Pass and carry a qualification recognised across the entire UK construction industry.

FAQ

Common Questions

No experience is needed at all. Our course is designed for complete beginners. You'll start with full machine familiarisation and build up systematically — mastering each task before moving on to the next.

CPCS stands for Construction Plant Competence Scheme — the most widely recognised plant operator card in the UK construction industry. Most principal contractors and groundwork companies require operators to hold a valid CPCS card. Completing our course and passing the end examination earns you this card.

We welcome students aged 17 to 45. Whether you're a school leaver looking for a skilled trade or someone making a career change later in life, our instructors have the experience to get you ready.

Training runs Monday to Friday, 8:00am until 3:30pm, over two consecutive weeks (10 training days). You'll need to be available for all days to complete the full programme and sit the CPCS examination.

Our pass rates are high because of how we train — mastering skills progressively rather than rushing. In the event you need a resit, we'll discuss your options with you directly. Our goal is to see every student qualify.

Yes. The course fee of £2,500 covers your full two-week training, all machine time across our 8 excavators, on-site PPE, and your CPCS examination. There are no hidden fees.
